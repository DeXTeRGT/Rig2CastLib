# Protocol provenance policy

Rig2Cast is an independent design, not a Hamlib port.

1. Prefer manufacturer-published manuals and behavior observed on owned hardware.
2. Hamlib may identify questions, commands, and quirks requiring independent verification.
3. Do not mechanically translate Hamlib functions, driver tables, comments, or tests.
4. Record the source and validation status for every implemented protocol feature.
5. Keep captured fixtures free of private data and third-party copyrighted documentation.
6. Flag any proposed direct reuse for license review before it enters Rig2Cast.

Driver records distinguish documentation evidence, simulator evidence, and verification against a named radio model and firmware version.
